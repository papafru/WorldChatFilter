# World Chat Filter v1.1.2 — Guild Recruitment Hotfix

This maintenance release fixes additional **German** and **Polish** guild advertisements that could slip through World Chat Filter **v1.1.1** on **OctoWoW 1.18** (Vanilla 1.12 addon API).

## Fixes

- **German social-guild invitations:** Better detect informal recruitment that promotes a relaxed community, leveling help, casual raiding and contact instructions. Examples include `Lust auf Community?`, `Casuals willkommen`, `Melde dich per PM`, `Meldet euch per PN` and `Schreib uns`. A raid schedule, class request or numbered open slot is no longer necessary when the combined guild/activity/invitation evidence is clear.
- **Polish guild recruitment:** Recognize direct guild announcements like `Polska gildia ... rekrutuje`, `poszukuje` or `przyjmuje` with raid/WoW activity—even without the words `graczy` or `osób`. Additional forms including `rajdy` and `raidy` are covered.
- **False-positive controls:** Detection combines guild identity, recruitment or contact intent, and gameplay/promotion context rather than blacklisting guild names, player names or isolated words. Ordinary discussions, references to past advertisements and explicitly unrelated invitations remain visible.

## Unchanged

- Filtering affects **World chat only**. Trade, General, LookingForGroup, Party, Raid, Guild and whispers are untouched.
- The Dark Arcane UI, personal player/phrase filters, Statistics, Help / Commands, World chat tint and saved settings are unchanged.
- No new dependencies or SavedVariables migration.
- **40/40 Lua regression test files passed** on the reviewed development build and final stable build, including multilingual messages, false-positive examples and World-only behavior.

## Download and installation

1. Download **[WorldChatFilter-v1.1.2.zip](https://github.com/papafru/WorldChatFilter/releases/download/v1.1.2/WorldChatFilter-v1.1.2.zip)** from this release's Assets. Use the packaged ZIP, not GitHub's autogenerated source ZIP.
2. **Close WoW**. Replace `Interface/AddOns/WorldChatFilter/` with the single `WorldChatFilter` folder contained in the archive (no extra nested folder).
3. Restart WoW. **Do not delete** your `WTF/.../SavedVariables/WorldChatFilter.lua` settings.

Addon managers can update using `https://github.com/papafru/WorldChatFilter`.
